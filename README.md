About Me

Hi, I'm Surabhi Byju, a Master of Information Systems Management student at Carnegie Mellon University (expected graduation: December 2026). I previously earned a Bachelor of Technology in Electronics Engineering from the University of Mumbai.
I enjoy working at the intersection of data, software engineering, and artificial intelligence. As a Software Engineer Intern at ServiceLink, I built an analytics dashboard tracking more than 20 KPIs, developed enterprise validation rules, and worked on REST APIs and user interfaces. Before that, I worked as an Associate Consultant at Ernst & Young, where I contributed to backend systems, document-processing automation, and predictive sales forecasting initiatives.

**What I Hope to Learn**
Through this course, I hope to strengthen my ability to turn data into compelling, accurate visual stories. In particular, I want to:
- Choose effective chart types for different questions, audiences, and datasets.
- Use color, typography, layout, and annotation deliberately to make insights easier to understand.
- Critique visualizations thoughtfully, including their strengths, limitations, and potential for misleading interpretations.
- Build clear narratives with data, rather than simply presenting charts or metrics.
- Create and publish interactive visualizations that support exploration while remaining accessible.
After graduation, I hope to apply these skills in software engineering, data analytics, and AI-focused roles where communicating technical results helps teams make better decisions.
